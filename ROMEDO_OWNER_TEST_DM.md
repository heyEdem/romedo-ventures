# Romedo Ventures — Owner Testing and Outreach Guide

## 1. Before sending

- Deploy the prototype to Vercel.
- Test homepage, product search, filters, product page, WhatsApp, phone, and mobile layout.
- Replace placeholder facts, phone numbers, branches, prices, hours, and product claims.
- Confirm the demo does not imply checkout, payments, stock quantities, or online ordering.
- Prepare one or two products the owner will recognize.

## 2. First message

```text
Hi [Name], I built a small prototype of a more modern Romedo Ventures website and thought you might find it interesting.

It is designed to help customers discover your products, find a branch, and contact the shop directly through WhatsApp or phone.

I’m sending you the link in case you have a few minutes to look around. I’d really value your honest feedback—especially anything that feels inaccurate, missing, or unlike how Romedo actually operates.
```

Send the link separately, with a short description of what to test rather than a technical explanation.

## 3. What to ask him to test

```text
Could you try searching for a product you sell, opening its details, and using the contact button?

I’d also like to know:
1. Can you find the products and categories you sell?
2. Does the website feel like Romedo?
3. Is any product, branch, or business information wrong?
4. What important thing is missing?
5. Would this be useful to your customers?
```

## 4. How to receive feedback

Listen before defending a design decision. Ask concrete follow-ups such as “Where would you expect to find that?” and “What would staff do in the real process?” Record corrections to product facts separately from ideas for future functionality.

If he asks who built it:

```text
I built it myself. I wanted to create something that could make it easier for customers to discover Romedo and contact the right shop.
```

## 5. Follow-up

If he responds positively:

```text
Thanks, that feedback is really helpful. My thinking is to start with the digital catalogue and then shape the next version around how Romedo actually manages products, branches, and enquiries.

If you’re interested, I’d be happy to show you what could be improved and discuss what would be most useful for the business.
```

If he does not respond, wait several days:

```text
Hi [Name], just following up on the Romedo website prototype I sent. No pressure—I’d still appreciate any quick feedback when you have time, even if it’s just the first thing you would change.
```

## 6. Conversation goal

The first contact is for learning, not selling a finished system. Move from “Does this look useful?” to “How does Romedo really manage products, branches, prices, and enquiries?” Discuss production scope, CMS choice, pricing, and implementation only after those workflows are understood.
